# Repeated punctuation checks

The deployment completed successfully after validation!!

Why did the secondary retry stop unexpectedly??

Did the final health check pass correctly?!

Was the cache ready before the rollout!?

The final result remained unclear after review...?!

The long-running operation eventually finished successfully…!

## Supported contexts

The deployment completed successfully after validation!

Why did the secondary retry stop unexpectedly?

The result remained inconclusive after review...

Use `?!` to represent mixed punctuation in the parser test.

```text
Really?!
```
